Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

HotDevJobs helps job seekers find roles and employers find candidates. This page explains the information used to run the site.

Information you provide

We store your account email and a protected password, your profile details, and information you submit through the site. A job seeker profile can include skills, work history, contact details, photos, and a resume. Recruiters can provide company details and job postings. We also store applications and related activity.

How it is used and shared

We use this information to provide accounts, show jobs, process applications, let recruiters review candidates who apply, and send verification or password reset emails. The site uses hosting and database services to operate. Email providers process the recipient address and message needed to deliver account emails.

Some AI features send the text described next to their consent checkbox to Google Gemini. For example, a resume comparison sends the text you review and the job requirements. When semantic search is enabled, selected profile fields and job text may be sent for embeddings. Uploaded resumes and contact fields are excluded from that embedding text, although information typed into other free-text fields may be included.

Google account access

The site's operator connects a Gmail account to send verification and password reset emails. HotDevJobs requests the gmail.send permission for this purpose. The app uses an OAuth refresh token to request access tokens and send these messages. It does not use this permission to read a mailbox. Job seekers and recruiters are not asked to connect their Google accounts.

Security, storage, and choices

Passwords and email verification codes are stored as hashes. The site uses cookies for sign-in sessions and, if selected, a remember-me login. Your account and submitted content are stored while the service needs them. You can update profile information in your account. To ask about access to or removal of your data, contact hotdevjobs.co@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this page when the service changes. The date above shows the latest version.
